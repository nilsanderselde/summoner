# Summoner DAW - Factory Stock Content & Sound Library Licensing Audit

All factory sounds, drum kits, multi-sample instruments, audio loops, impulse responses, wavetables, and demo projects bundled with or generated by Summoner DAW are **100% Royalty-Free** for use in your musical, commercial, film, gaming, streaming, and multimedia productions.

## 1. Summary of Rights

You are granted a worldwide, non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to:
- Use all factory loops, drum samples, instruments, and presets in commercial recordings, film scores, video games, broadcasts, and live performances.
- Modify, chop, pitch, reverse, and process the audio in any creative manner.
- Release commercial music created with these assets on streaming platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, etc.) without paying royalties or providing attribution to the Summoner DAW developers.

### Restrictions
- You may **NOT** re-package, distribute, or sell the raw audio files, loops, impulse responses, or multisamples as a standalone or derivative commercial sample library, soundfont, or sound effect pack.
